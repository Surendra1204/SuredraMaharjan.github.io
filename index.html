About me

I’m a PhD researcher focused on hydroclimate extremes, water resources, and machine learning. My work combines remote sensing, reanalysis/climate datasets, and statistical learning to understand and predict droughts, floods, and compound events.

I’m especially interested in building reproducible, scalable workflows in Python/R and translating scientific results into practical tools for risk assessment and decision support.
